Escalation Decision

Classification:
Confirmed phishing / social-engineering attempt involving abuse of a legitimate Meta Business Manager workflow.

Initial Severity:
Medium

Reason for Escalation:
- The message originated from legitimate Meta infrastructure and passed SPF, DKIM, and DMARC.
- Attacker-controlled text was inserted into a legitimate Business Manager partner request.
- The lure attempted to move the recipient toward a Messenger identifier.
- Traditional sender-authentication controls alone would not reliably identify this activity.

Compromise Status:
No confirmed compromise.

The available evidence does not show that the recipient:
- interacted with the Messenger lure,
- approved the partner request,
- disclosed credentials or MFA codes,
- or lost access to a Meta Business account.

SOC Decision:
Escalate for phishing investigation and user/account validation. Further response should depend on whether the recipient interacted with the request.
