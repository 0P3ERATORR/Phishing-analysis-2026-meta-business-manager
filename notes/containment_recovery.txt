Containment and Recovery Recommendations

If the recipient did NOT interact with the lure:
- Remove/quarantine the phishing email.
- Warn the recipient not to follow the Messenger lure or approve the partner request.
- Review other recent Meta Business Manager partner requests for similar wording or identifiers.
- Monitor the affected account for unusual partner invitations, login activity, or business-asset changes.
- Report the malicious partner request through Meta's official reporting channels.

If the recipient DID interact with the lure:
- Revoke any unauthorized Meta Business Manager partner access immediately.
- Review business assets, Pages, ad accounts, pixels, and permissions for unauthorized changes.
- Reset the affected account password.
- Revoke active sessions and re-authenticate trusted devices.
- Review and reconfigure MFA if credentials or MFA codes may have been disclosed.
- Investigate recent advertising activity and payment changes for unauthorized use.
- Preserve relevant Meta activity logs and account evidence for further investigation.

Recovery:
- Confirm that only legitimate administrators and partners retain access.
- Validate account recovery information and MFA configuration.
- Continue monitoring for follow-on social-engineering attempts.
